About AgroSky AI

🌾 Our Mission

AgroSky AI is an innovative AI-powered platform designed to revolutionize agriculture through data-driven decision making. We leverage advanced machine learning models to provide accurate crop recommendations and yield predictions, helping farmers optimize their agricultural practices.

🤖 Technology

Our platform uses state-of-the-art Random Forest algorithms for both classification (crop recommendation) and regression (yield prediction). The models are trained on comprehensive agricultural datasets, considering factors like soil nutrients (N, P, K), temperature, humidity, rainfall, and soil pH to deliver precise predictions.

🎯 Key Features

  • ✅ AI-powered crop recommendation based on soil and climate data
  • ✅ Accurate yield prediction in kg per hectare
  • ✅ Top 5 crop recommendations with suitability scores
  • ✅ Visual analytics with interactive charts
  • ✅ Real-time predictions with detailed insights

👥 Our Team

🎓 Project Context

This project is part of the Infosys Springboard Internship Program, demonstrating the practical application of machine learning and artificial intelligence in solving real-world agricultural challenges.
