🌱 About the System

AgriYield is an AI-powered agricultural decision-support platform designed to assist farmers and agricultural planners in making informed, data-driven crop decisions.

By analyzing soil nutrients (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium), climatic conditions (temperature, humidity, rainfall), and soil pH, the system intelligently:

  • 📈 Predicts the expected crop yield (kg per hectare) for a selected crop
  • 📊 Recommends the Top 5 most suitable crops for the given field conditions

The system combines Machine Learning–based crop suitability analysis with crop-specific yield prediction, reducing guesswork and enabling sustainable, productivity-focused farming.
