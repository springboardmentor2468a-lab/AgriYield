About the System

The Crop Recommendation System helps users identify suitable crops based on soil nutrients and climatic conditions. It is designed to support better crop selection by providing clear and data-driven recommendations through an interactive interface.

By analyzing soil nutrients (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium), climatic factors (temperature, humidity, rainfall), and soil pH, the system evaluates field conditions and suggests crops that are best suited for cultivation.

🌾 Recommends suitable crops for the given field conditions
📊 Visualizes crop suitability using confidence-based indicators

This approach reduces guesswork in crop selection and supports more informed, efficient, and sustainable farming decisions.
