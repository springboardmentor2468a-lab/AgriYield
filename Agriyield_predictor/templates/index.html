🌾 About AgriPredict & Protect

AgriPredict & Protect is an AI-powered smart agriculture system designed to help farmers make better decisions using Machine Learning and Deep Learning technologies. This system focuses on Crop Yield Prediction and Plant Leaf Disease Detection with Treatment & Prevention.

📊 Crop Yield Prediction System

This module predicts crop production based on soil nutrients and environmental factors such as Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), temperature, humidity, rainfall, and pH value.

  • ✔ Machine Learning based prediction
  • ✔ Output in production units (tons/kg)
  • ✔ Helps farmers choose best crop
  • ✔ Supports sustainable farming

🍃 Plant Leaf Disease Detection

The disease prediction system uses a trained Deep Learning model that analyzes plant leaf images and identifies diseases with high accuracy.

  • ✔ Image-based disease prediction
  • ✔ Treatment recommendations
  • ✔ Disease prevention tips
  • ✔ Works on real leaf images
